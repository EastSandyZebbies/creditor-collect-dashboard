Account Scoring & Prioritisation
🥇
Tier 1 — High Fit: CW clients 12mo+, finance/professional services/retail, 10–150 staff, MYOB/Xero users. High AR volume, manual follow-up pain visible.
🥈
Tier 2 — Medium Fit: CW clients 6–12mo, SME <50 staff, no dedicated AR team. Good for automation starter pitch.
🥉
Tier 3 — Monitor: New CW clients <6mo or very small (<10 staff). Nurture only — don't push Collect yet.
📊
Scoring signals: CW overdue trends, days-since-last-Collect-touch, industry AR benchmarks, CSM flags on AR pain.
Expansion Sales Motion
STEP 1Warm intro via CSM. No cold outreach — leverage the CW relationship. CSM flags high-fit accounts.
STEP 2Discovery call. How do they chase overdue invoices today? Hours per week? What's their DSO?
STEP 3Tailored demo. Show Collect solving their specific pain — email/SMS workflows, Call Console, one-click debt escalation.
STEP 4ROI proposal. Build a DSO model — 13% DSO reduction on $500K receivables = $65K cash freed. Pays for itself in weeks.
CLOSE14-day free trial. Low friction — let the product prove itself. Convert to $1,100 MRR on completion.
Key Objections & Responses
ObjectionResponse
"We already chase invoices manually — it works fine."Ask: how many hours per week? Collect automates up to 85% of that. What would your team do with that time back?
"We pay for CW already — why another cost?"Reframe as ROI: 13% DSO reduction on $500K receivables = $65K cash freed. Collect pays for itself in weeks, not months.
"Our accounting software already sends reminders."Collect goes beyond reminders — workflows, Call Console, debt escalation, AR reporting. Accounting software stops at the invoice.
"We don't have time to implement new software."Xero/MYOB sync in minutes. Best-practice templates out of the box. Most customers are live and collecting in under a week.
